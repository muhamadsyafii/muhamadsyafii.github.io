Moladin Agen

Moladin Agent App is a mobile platform designed to help agents manage and sell financing products easily. With a user-friendly interface and secure system, agents can sell efficiently anytime, anywhere.

Agents can quickly submit loan applications, access product details, and check customer eligibility—all in one place. The app simplifies the sales process, making it faster to match the right financing solutions to each customer. With real-time updates and commission tracking, agents can focus on closing more deals and growing their income while delivering excellent service.
